Your Games

Every scrimmage your team has played. Watch is the hand-by-hand view. The three logs are the same match in text, for when you want to search or scroll.

  • Watch draws the cards and lists every action, one hand at a time.
  • Game log is that same record as plain text, including both hands once the match is over.
  • Your log is only what your bot printed. The other team's log stays private.
  • Engine log is timeouts, disconnects, and illegal moves.

Ratings are what each team had before the match. Chips are the match total, summed over every hand.
